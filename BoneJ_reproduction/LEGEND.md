**Fig. X | Agentic-J reproduces a published BoneJ analysis of trabecular bone architecture.**
Agentic-J was given the segmented third-lumbar-vertebra volumes of 66 Philippine earthworm mice (11 species, 5 genera;
Smith et al. 2023, Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.k3j9kd5c7) and a biological question, without naming BoneJ or any algorithm.
**a**, Species means computed by Agentic-J against the published values (Table 2 of Smith et al.) for bone volume fraction
(BV/TV), trabecular thickness (Tb.Th), trabecular separation (Tb.Sp) and connectivity density (Conn.D). Bars, s.d. across
specimens; dashed line, identity; CCC, Lin's concordance correlation coefficient; mean |Δ|, mean absolute deviation in % of
the published mean. Open diamonds: Tb.Sp re-measured for two complete species (*Soricomys kalinga*, *Chrotomys
mindorensis*) with BoneJ's "Mask thickness maps" option disabled. **b**, Per-species deviation from the published means;
grey band, ±1%. BV/TV, Tb.Th and Conn.D deviate by at most 0.9%. Tb.Sp is 5–10% higher in every species; with masking
disabled it matches the published values to within 0.05%, showing that the authors measured Tb.Sp without, and Tb.Th with,
this option, a setting the methods do not report. **c**, Allometric scaling exponents (slope of log10 metric on log10
body mass across the 11 species means, ±95% CI) from the published and the reproduced values. **d**, Each metric against
body mass: per-specimen values from Agentic-J (light points), species means (published, open circles; Agentic-J, filled)
and log–log fits. One deposited specimen (FMNH 190962) contains no image data, so 66 of the 67 published specimens could
be analysed.
