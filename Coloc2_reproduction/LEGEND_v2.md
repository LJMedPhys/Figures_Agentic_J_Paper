**Fig. X | Agentic-J reproduces a published Coloc 2 colocalization result.**
Wide-field images (100×) of *Drosophila* S2 cells stained for Su(Hw), phosphorylated H2Av (γH2Av) and DAPI, in isotonic
medium (6 fields) or after 250 mM NaCl osmotic stress (11 fields), from Simmons et al. 2022 (*PLoS Genet*,
doi:10.1371/journal.pgen.1010396, Fig. 5A–C; data Dryad doi:10.5061/dryad.k98sf7m8x). Agentic-J was asked whether osmotic
stress increases nuclear γH2Av–Su(Hw) overlap, with background subtraction, DAPI-defined nuclear regions and Costes
thresholds specified but no plugin named; it chose Fiji's Coloc 2. **a**, The fields closest to each group mean (isotonic field 6, NaCl field 1). Left, Su(Hw) (magenta) and γH2Av (green); right, DAPI of the same region with the nuclear mask Agentic-J derived from it (yellow outline; corrected three times by the user during the interactive session). Each channel uses the same display range in both fields. Scale bar, 10 µm (0.0645 µm per pixel). **b**, Pearson's R above the Costes thresholds per image field, published (values
from the authors' Figure 5 data sheet) and from Agentic-J. Bars, mean ± s.d. **c**, Agentic-J field values against
published field values, sorted within each condition because the published sheet does not identify the image behind each
value; dashed line, identity. Increase under NaCl: published +0.490 (95% bootstrap CI +0.31 to +0.68; exact permutation
P = 1.6 × 10⁻⁴), Agentic-J +0.498 (+0.35 to +0.65; P = 8.1 × 10⁻⁵).
